Popular answers

The questions travelers ask most, answered with station data. (この欄は普通の文章なので、Google と AI が読めます)

Shinjuku + Asakusa → Kanda

Both without a transfer: JR Chuo Line to Shinjuku, Ginza Line to Asakusa. A small, easy station.

Disney + Shibuya → Hatchobori

No station does both directly. Hatchobori is direct to Disney and one transfer to Shibuya, with a calm station.

Climbing Mt. Fuji → Shinjuku, then Kawaguchiko

Night before: Shinjuku for the bus to the 5th Station. After: an onsen ryokan at Lake Kawaguchi.

Early Shinkansen to Kyoto → Nihombashi

A short ride to Tokyo Station without its crowds. Shinagawa is the other good pick.
